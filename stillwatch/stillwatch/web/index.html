Stillwatch is watching.

It learns when someone normally moves around their own home, hour by hour, and notices when they stop. The hard part is not spotting silence. It is telling she went out from she stopped, because both look the same from the outside.

A recorded day, so it can be seen working before this home has a history of its own.
